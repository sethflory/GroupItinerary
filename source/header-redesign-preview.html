Real-World References

🦉 Duolingo

Leaderboard pattern: Weekly leagues with 10-15 competitors at similar level. Small groups feel achievable, not overwhelming.

What we're borrowing: Trip scoreboard with ranked travelers, recent activity feed, share to social.

Gamification Leaderboard Badges

💬 Slack / Discord

Activity indicators: Unread badges, "typing..." indicators, active call banners that surface what needs attention.

What we're borrowing: Activity bar showing active games and open polls with quick-join CTAs.

Real-time Notifications Quick Actions

🗺️ Wanderlog

Clean itinerary: Day-by-day layout, map integration, visually appealing without clutter. Rated 10/10 for UX.

What we're borrowing: Simplified header, content-first approach, day quick-jump navigation.

Travel App Clean UI Maps

✈️ Flighty

Contextual status: Shows different info pre-flight vs during vs after. "Now" focused with live updates.

What we're borrowing: Context bar that adapts to trip phase (countdown → live status → memories).

Status Bar Live Updates Phase Aware

📱 iOS/Android Settings

Slide-out menu: Organized sections, toggles for preferences, info at bottom. Familiar pattern.

What we're borrowing: Menu drawer with sections, photo ribbon toggle, quick info footer.

Navigation Settings Toggles

🏃 Strava

Social competition: Segment leaderboards, kudos, activity feed. Makes exercise social and competitive.

What we're borrowing: Points from activities, friendly competition, share achievements.

Social Competition Activity Feed
